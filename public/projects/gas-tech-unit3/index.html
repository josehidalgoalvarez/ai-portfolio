Key Safety & Service Takeaways

The top 5 most critical concepts for field application.

1. Specific Gravity is Critical

Natural Gas (S.G. ~0.60) rises. Propane (S.G. ~1.52) sinks and pools in low areas.

2. Know Your Pressures: PSIG vs. W.C.

PSIG for high pressure. W.C. for appliances. Key: 1 PSI ≈ 27.7" W.C.

3. CO is the Silent Killer

Legal max 400 ppm (air-free), but >100 ppm is unsafe.

4. Trust Your Nose (Mercaptan)

The "rotten egg" smell indicates a leak. Never ignore it.

5. Gases Behave Predictably (Gas Laws)

Boyle's Law: P₁V₁ = P₂V₂. Doubling pressure halves volume.

Understanding the Airflow

This diagram illustrates the critical airflow paths in a standard natural draft gas furnace:

  • Combustion Air: Air that mixes directly with the gas at the burner to support the flame.
  • Dilution Air: Air that enters through the draft hood to cool the hot flue gases and assist in creating a stable draft.
  • Circulating Air: The household air (cold return) that is heated and distributed to the rooms.
