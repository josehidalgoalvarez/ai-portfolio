Professional Gas Furnace Maintenance

This SOP provides comprehensive procedures for servicing high-efficiency condensing furnaces, ensuring regulatory compliance, optimal performance, and customer safety. All procedures follow manufacturer specifications and TSSA requirements.

Critical Performance Metric

Every furnace service must verify temperature rise to ensure safe, efficient operation and warranty compliance.

Target Temperature Rise
40-60°F
Must match nameplate range

Regulatory Compliance

All work must comply with CSA B149.1-25, Ontario Regulation 212/01, and TSSA requirements. Only certified G.2 or G.3 technicians may perform this work.

Manufacturer Standards

Follow all manufacturer specifications for disassembly, cleaning, reassembly, and commissioning. Maintain warranty compliance.

Professional Documentation

Document all findings, measurements, and actions taken. Provide customer with service report and maintenance recommendations.

Safety & Regulatory Compliance

⚠️ CRITICAL SAFETY WARNINGS

ELECTRICAL HAZARD: Disconnect electrical supply to furnace before attempting service. Failure to do so can cause electrical shock resulting in personal injury or death.

EXPLOSION HAZARD: If gas supply fails to shut off, close manual gas valve to appliance. Never purge gas lines into combustion chamber. Never use torches or any ignition source for checking gas leaks. Failure to follow these warnings can cause explosion or fire.

MANDATORY WAIT: After turning gas valve to OFF, wait minimum 5 minutes before proceeding with any work. This allows residual gas to dissipate.

TEMPERATURE RISE: Operating unit with temperature rise outside nameplate range will void warranty and could cause heat exchanger damage, intermittent operation, injury, or death.

TSSA Certification Requirements

G.3 Certificate Holders: May perform basic maintenance including filter changes, visual inspections, and cleaning under direct supervision of G.2 or G.1 certificate holder.

G.2 Certificate Holders: May install, inspect, alter, purge, activate, repair, service, or remove natural gas or propane appliances up to 400,000 BTU/hr input.

G.1 Certificate Holders: May perform all functions on appliances of any BTU input. Required for systems over 400,000 BTU/hr.

Reference: Ontario Regulation 215/01, Sections 20-22

Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

  • CSA-approved safety glasses (mandatory during all work)
  • Work gloves (protection from sharp edges and heat retention)
  • Non-sparking LED flashlight for interior inspection
  • Appropriate clothing (no loose items near moving parts)

Gas Leak Detection Protocol

Approved Methods: Use only approved leak detection solutions (soap and water solution or commercial leak detector). NEVER use open flame.

If Leak Detected: Immediately shut off gas supply at manual valve. Tag valve to prevent reopening. Notify customer and repair before proceeding with any startup.

Manifold Pressure Check: Verify manifold pressure matches nameplate specification before and after service.
