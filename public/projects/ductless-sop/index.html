Customer Interaction Protocol

Follow these scripts to guide the customer conversation, build trust, and demonstrate professionalism from arrival to departure.

1. Initial Contact & Diagnosis

Greeting: `"Good morning, my name is [Name] from [Company]. I'm here to look at the mini-split that wasn't cooling properly."`

Set Expectations: `"First, I'll do a 15-minute evaluation. Once I have a clear diagnosis, I'll explain my findings and the next steps before I perform any work."`

2. Explaining the Solution

Use an Analogy: `"The good news is it's a maintenance issue. Think of your AC trying to breathe through a stuffy mask. The internal coil and fan are clogged, so it can't move air effectively."`

Quote the Work: `"The solution is our deep cleaning service. This will restore cooling power and improve air quality. The price is [Price]. Would you like me to proceed?"`

3. Final Debrief & Value Offer

Provide Preventative Tips: `"To help keep it running efficiently, I recommend rinsing the mesh filters once a month. It's easy and makes a huge difference."`

Pitch Service Agreement: `"To make sure you don't have to worry about this again, we offer a service membership. We come out annually for a full deep cleaning and inspection. It saves you money on energy bills and prevents bigger problems."`
